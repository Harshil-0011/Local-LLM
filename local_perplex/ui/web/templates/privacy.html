The Privacy Manifesto

Local Perplex was built with a single mission: to provide world-class research capabilities without sacrificing human privacy. Unlike cloud-based search engines, we do not profile your interests, sell your search history, or use your data to train proprietary models. Your research is yours alone.

Every query, every analyzed document, and every generated response stays within your hardware. This is not just a tool; it is a fortress for your curiosity.
